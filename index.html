WHO WE ARE

A student-run community built for people who'd rather build than just learn about it.

Nexus brings together students from every department who share one thing in common: curiosity about technology. We run hands-on workshops, ship real projects, and send teams to hackathons across the country — all driven by members, for members.

Our Mission

Make hands-on tech education accessible to every student, regardless of major.

Our Vision

A campus where every student graduates with something real they've shipped.

Community Impact

1,500+ members across 40+ departments, learning and building together.

Learning Culture

Peer-led workshops, open critiques, and a no-question-is-dumb ethos.
